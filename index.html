FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What type of employees can you help us recruit?

We assist businesses with recruitment for a range of positions, from entry-level and administrative roles to experienced professionals and leadership positions.

How does your recruitment process work?

We first understand your requirements, search for suitable candidates, screen potential applicants and then present suitable candidates for your consideration.

Do you work with job seekers?

Yes. We connect qualified candidates with suitable employment opportunities based on their skills, experience and career goals.

How long does recruitment take?

The timeline depends on the role, number of positions and specific requirements. We aim to make the process as efficient as possible.

How can I get started?

Simply contact Elite Workforce Agency through WhatsApp. Tell us what you need and a member of our team will guide you through the next steps.
